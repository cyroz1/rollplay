BROWSER-NATIVE MUSIC TOOL

Bring FL Studio projects to life.

ROLLPLAY is a private, browser-based FLP visualizer for producers who want to turn a piano roll into a shareable music video. Load a project, shape its layers, and export the result without uploading your files.

Visualize FLP files

Read tempo, patterns, arrangements, and notes from FL Studio project files and preview them as an animated piano roll.

Style every layer

Customize colors, gradients, note motion, percussion shapes, playhead effects, backgrounds, and parallax depth.

Export MIDI and MP4

Download a standards-compliant MIDI file or render a portrait, landscape, or square MP4 music video in supported browsers.

HOW IT WORKS

From project to visualizer in three steps.

  1. 01

    Load your project

    Drop an FL Studio .flp file into ROLLPLAY. Add a finished audio file when you want it included in the video.

  2. 02

    Shape the motion

    Choose the frame, background, colors, animations, layer order, and playhead style while previewing playback.

  3. 03

    Export your work

    Download MIDI or render an MP4 with your chosen resolution, frame rate, and maximum file size.

ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions.

What is ROLLPLAY?

ROLLPLAY is a browser-based FL Studio FLP visualizer that turns project notes and arrangements into an animated piano-roll preview and shareable music video.

How does ROLLPLAY handle my files?

ROLLPLAY parses FLP projects and audio in your browser. Your project, audio, and generated exports are not uploaded to a server.

What can I export from ROLLPLAY?

You can download standards-compliant MIDI files and render H.264/AAC MP4 videos in supported browsers. Exports can be portrait, landscape, or square at 24, 30, or 60 FPS.

Which browsers support MP4 rendering?

MP4 rendering requires a Chromium-based browser with WebCodecs, H.264 VideoEncoder, and AAC AudioEncoder support. Project parsing, preview, playback, and MIDI export work in current browsers.
